# Contrato entre desenho e implementação

Use this internal contract to connect a commercial design to implementation within Kommo Engineer. Commands, API schemas, field maps and account-specific runbooks stay in the account-engineering references and the client project.

When the request reaches Kommo-specific execution or technical verification:

1. Consult the integrated technical module at `../crm/account-engineering.md` as the technical authority and follow its procedures, available tools, and account safeguards.
2. Carry forward the approved objective, exact account and target scope, relevant evidence, accepted design, exclusions, required readback, and rollback expectation.
3. Do not infer user approval from this proposal or from the existence of an integration. Confirm the user's authorization covers the exact action.
4. Bring observed technical findings and readback into the final report; do not claim success beyond the evidence actually observed.

The account-engineering guidance is native content of Kommo Engineer; it requires no separate Kommo skill. If a needed reference or current-session tool is unavailable, explain the gap and do not improvise Kommo API/UI details from other CRM sources.
