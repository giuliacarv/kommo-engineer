# Sales operations analyses

Use only the subsection that matches the user request. These are analysis patterns; examples and thresholds from other CRM plugins are not universal rules.

## Pipeline review

Choose the requested team, segment, stage, owner, or time window. Report the record population and timestamp, stage/age distribution, next-step or close-date gaps, and evidence-backed risks. Keep an opportunity flag distinct from a prediction. Cite each material claim to a current record or calculation.

## CRM hygiene

Check identity completeness, duplicate candidates, stale ownership, stage/close-date consistency, missing next steps, and field conflicts within the authorized scope. Show examples and criteria. Present proposed cleanup before writes. Do not conflate blank data with a false value or overwrite ambiguous identity.

## Win/loss review

Combine outcome counts with a clearly bounded qualitative sample where available. Show sample size and coverage by segment, date, and source. Distinguish recorded loss reasons from inferred causes. Do not claim causal effects from a small or biased sample.

## Deal review

Assess evidence for customer need, decision process, stakeholders, impact, timing, competition, commercial fit, and next step where relevant to the user's methodology. Classify each item as supported, contradicted, or unknown. Use this to identify diligence questions and risks; do not copy static probability increments into the forecast.

## Forecast review

Use the organization's categories and prior-period snapshots. Reconcile amount, close period, stage, evidence, and owner; distinguish committed values from possible values under local definitions. Explain coverage, concentration, movement, and missing inputs. Do not submit or overwrite an official forecast unless requested and authorized.

## Lead triage

Apply the user's actual ICP, intent signals, geography, segment rules, deduplication, routing, and service capacity. Return reason codes and supporting evidence. Unknown information should remain unknown or enter a review queue. Do not copy P0/P1/P2 percentages, priority labels, response SLAs, or routing rules from source material.

## Account tiering and context

Use agreed fit/engagement criteria and a transparent evidence window. Keep sourced facts, internal interactions, CRM fields, and hypotheses distinguishable. Show how weights or cutoffs were chosen; if they are not calibrated, use descriptive groups and propose validation rather than false precision.

## Opportunity updates and expansion

Map an opportunity or expansion idea to current customer evidence, owner, next action, and status. Present proposed field changes explicitly. Write only the fields and records authorized by the user; read them back after updating. Treat possible whitespace as a question for discovery, not presumed demand or pipeline.
