# Handoff to the Kommo technical specialist

This strategic skill does not duplicate Kommo commands, API schemas, field maps, or account-specific operational runbooks.

When the request reaches Kommo-specific execution or technical verification:

1. Consult the integrated technical module at `../kommo-crm/SKILL.md` as the technical authority and follow its procedures, available tools, and account safeguards.
2. Pass it the approved objective, exact account and target scope, relevant evidence, accepted design, exclusions, required readback, and rollback expectation.
3. Do not ask it to infer user approval from this proposal or from the existence of an integration. Confirm the user's authorization covers the exact action.
4. Bring its observed technical findings and readback into the final report; do not claim success beyond the evidence it returned.

The technical module is bundled with Kommo Engineer. If a needed reference or current-session tool is unavailable, explain the gap and do not improvise Kommo API/UI details from other CRM sources.
