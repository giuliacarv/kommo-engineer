# Customer success and expansion workflows

Use when the commercial process continues after the sale: onboarding, adoption, value realization, renewal, risk, support handoff, or account expansion.

## Map the customer outcome

Start with the customer's desired outcome, the agreed success measure, the owner, and the evidence that value was achieved. Map onboarding and milestones only where they are present in the organization's actual motion. Identify risks from observed adoption, service, communication, renewal, and stakeholder signals; distinguish risk evidence from a predicted churn event.

## Health, risk, and expansion

Use only agreed signals and thresholds. If there is no calibrated health model, provide a transparent signal summary rather than an invented score. Attribute each signal to a source and date, and identify missing visibility. An expansion hypothesis needs customer evidence, a plausible outcome, an owner, and a next discovery action before it becomes a CRM opportunity.

## Handoffs and communication

Name the single accountable owner and receiving role for each handoff. Keep customer communications aligned with consent, account policy, and the user's authorization. Stop or revalidate scheduled outreach when a reply, support issue, owner change, opt-out, renewal event, or other locally defined cancellation condition occurs.

## Using customer-success source material

The supplied Claude for Customer Success repository is a reference implementation with multiple plugins and skills, not a Kommo package to install wholesale. Its lifecycle framing may inform this module, but its named frameworks, agents, connectors, workflows, and prompt wording are not imported as defaults.
